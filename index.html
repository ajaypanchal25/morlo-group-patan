ABOUT MORLO GROUP

Morlo Group Patan is a premier professional event and entertainment group. We organize Raas Garba, Bhajan, Live Shows, High-Bass DJ, Private Royal Events, Government Events, Ramel and sublime cultural celebrations.

Under the visionary leadership of Shailesh Goswami and powered by exceptional musical talent such as star lead singer Mansi Dave, Morlo Group Patan crafts monumental cultural celebrations with flawless sound engineering, spiritual devotion, and unmatched grandeur.
