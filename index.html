ABOUT MORLO GROUP

Morlo Group Patan is a professional event and entertainment group. We organize Raas Garba, Bhajan, Live Shows, DJ, Private Events, Government Events, Ramel and other cultural programs.

Under the visionary leadership of Shailesh Goswami and driven by exceptional musical talent such as lead singer Mansi Dave, Morlo Group Patan crafts monumental cultural celebrations with flawless sound engineering, spiritual devotion, and unmatched royal grandeur.
